# Benchmark notes (ideas only: never copy a post, caption or design, never quote)

## 3 Nov 2026: what wins for the benchmarks (stats/benchmarks.json, last 50 posts each)

**Karlton Dennis (764K IG followers).** Feed posts (carousels/images) median 1,124 likes vs Reels 397, on an established account. His winners:
- Side-by-side comparisons with almost no words: "Same paycheck. Very different vibes." (5.3K likes). Same idea: "Same $1M income, wildly different results depending on where you live" (3.1K), "Two businesses, same profit, completely different tax" (1.3K).
- Curiosity about something people see every day: "Why do so many business owners drive big SUVs?" (2.7K), "Golf isn't a write-off on its own, but a business built around it can be" (4.6K).
- A personal "here's what I do" story with real numbers (child on payroll, retirement account): 4.5K and 1.9K.
- Giant save-able list: "41 ways to lower your 2026 taxable income. Most people only know two or three. SAVE this and count how many you use."
- "Before your spouse takes that job, run this math" (1.3K): a decision people are about to make, with a number.
- Motivational/mindset image posts top the list (10.7K) but don't fit an anonymous, education-only brand.
- Weakest: event promos, behind-the-scenes, abstract statements ("The tax code is a list of things the government wants you to do").

## What we take from it (Australian, compliant)
1. **Covers that promise something:** comparison ("Same $90K. Very different tax."), curiosity ("Why do so many tradies drive dual-cab utes?"), a giant list ("27 ways to lower your 2026–27 taxable income"), a ranking ("Ranking tax moves that feel illegal but aren't"), a milestone ("What changes when your business hits $75K").
2. **Fewer words on the cover, more contrast:** two columns, a tier board, a price tag, red vs green. Multi-colour, not one-colour text slides.
3. **End with a question** ("Which one do you use? Agree or disagree?") on non-keyword posts to pull comments; keyword (ABN/CLAIM) only when a lead magnet is the CTA.
4. **Carousels are not dead** once there are followers (Karlton's feed beats his Reels), so keep making them, but cut every one into a Reel while follower count is near zero.
5. Ideas needing ATO verification before use: car limit and dual-cab ute treatment, paying family members in a business (must be reasonable for work actually done), the 2026–27 taxable-income list.
